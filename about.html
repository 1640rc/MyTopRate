About Us

What MyTopRate is

MyTopRate is a free tool for finding the highest-rated businesses near you. Type in a zip code or share your location, pick a business type, and it pulls back nearby results sorted by Google rating, review count, or a blended "Recommended" score — so you don't have to dig through a map full of pins to find the place people actually vouch for.

Why it exists

The idea started from a simple frustration: standard map searches are sorted by distance or relevance, not by quality. Often the best-reviewed spot in an area is buried a few results down. MyTopRate exists to flip that — rating and reputation first, distance second.

Who's behind it

MyTopRate is built and maintained by a single developer, Roberto, based in Florida. It's an independent, self-funded project — not backed by a company or a data broker. All the business data shown comes directly from Google; MyTopRate itself doesn't collect or sell information about the businesses or the people searching for them (see the Privacy Policy for details).

How it stays free

The site is free to use and may be supported by advertising. Displaying an ad for a business doesn't mean MyTopRate has vetted or endorsed it — see the Terms of Service for how that works.
