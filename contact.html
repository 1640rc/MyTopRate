What to contact us about

  • Incorrect or outdated business info — ratings, hours, and addresses come directly from Google, so corrections are usually fastest made on the business's own Google Business Profile. If something looks broken on our end specifically, let us know.
  • Bugs or something not working — tell us what you searched, what device/browser you're on, and what happened.
  • Privacy or Terms questions — see the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service first; email us if something's still unclear.
  • Advertising or partnership inquiries.

Response time

This isn't a staffed support line, so please allow a few days for a reply, especially for non-urgent requests.
